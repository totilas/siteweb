Since joining Paris-Dauphine, I had the opportunity to present my work in several seminars and workshops around Paris:

- **[Young Statisticians and Probabilists Day](https://ysp2026.sciencesconf.org/?lang=fr)** (January 14, 2026, Institut Henri Poincaré): I spoke in the privacy session of this day organized by the young statisticians group of the Société Française de Statistique.
- **INRIA SODA team seminar** (April 28, 2026): _Optimizing Performative Learning_.
- **Laboratoire de Mathématiques d'Orsay** (May 7, 2026): _Optimizing Performative Learning_.
- **[Data Transitions seminar](https://cermics-lab.enpc.fr/seminaires/data-transitions/)** (June 18, 2026, École des Ponts, CERMICS): _Optimizing Performative Learning_. This seminar series on data science is open to a broad audience, so it was a nice popularization exercise: starting from concrete examples such as hiring algorithms, I explained how deployed models change the data they learn from, and why this matters for trustworthy machine learning.
- **[Crossroads of AI & Society Workshop](https://aaforml.com/2026/)** (July 16, 2026, organized by Rice University in Paris): _Various Ways to Set Privacy Budgets_. An interdisciplinary workshop on incentives, privacy and fairness, where it was nice to see some Simons faces again.
