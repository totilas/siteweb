I was invited to give a talk at [Learning and Optimization in Luminy (LOL)](https://www.cirm-math.fr/Schedule/display.php?id_renc=3630), a week-long workshop at CIRM gathering young researchers and experts around generative modelling, optimization for deep learning and trustworthy machine learning. My talk, _Optimizing Performative Learning_, introduced the performative learning setting and covered my two papers on the topic: [Optimal Classification under Performative Distribution Shift](@/research/2024-11-04-performative-classification/index.md) and [Optimal Regularization for Performative Learning](@/research/2025-10-15-reg-versus-perf/index.md).

Back in Luminy four years after my [first visit](@/talks/2022-11-10-cirm.md), with the same view on the Calanques.
