I am currently a Postdoc at [ISTA](https://ista.ac.at/home) working with [Christoph Lampert](https://cvml.ista.ac.at/) and [Marco Mondelli](http://marcomondelli.com/) on Trustworthy Machine Learning. Previously, my PhD was supervised by [Aurélien Bellet](http://researchers.lille.inria.fr/abellet/) in [Magnet](https://team.inria.fr/magnet/) team, on the topic of *Differential Privacy for Decentralized Learning* and I was awarded L'Oreal-UNESCO Yound Talents awards for my doctoral work.
I was previously civil-servant for my studies at ENS Lyon in Computer Science. I graduated from a dual-degree programme as a civil engineer at MINES ParisTech. I have a Master’s of Research degree in applied mathematics for machine learning (MVA).

I chose to work on machine learning because of its social impacts and the thrilling idea to learn from high dimensional data. I graduated from a Master Degree of Philosophy at Paris 1 Panthéon Sorbonne. I did various projects for the popularization of science, and I seek to continue to explore philosophical and ourtreach part of machine learning in the future. I am particularly drawn to privacy, fairness, accountability and regulatory issues with regard to ML from a mathematical point of view.

As a woman in engineering, I am a firm believer in diversity and I strive to advocate at a personal level by volunteering in projects that promote girls and women in STEM industries. I participate to a lot of local actions, but currently I chose to focus on a big event: [EGMO 2026](https://www.egmo.org/egmos/egmo15/) in France. The European Girls’ Mathematical Olympiad is an international mathematical contest of a very high level, aiming at encouraging girls to discover and explore their mathematical talent. If you are interested by sponsoring the event, please contact me.
