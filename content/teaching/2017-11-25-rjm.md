I co-organized the first Rendez-vous des jeunes mathématiciennes in Lyon, with the financial support of Animath. 30 girls in high school met during three intensive days to discover Olympic mathematics. I was responsible of both mathematical (tutorial, lectures) and non mathematical parts (housing, food, games, workshop on stereotypes). I wrote an [article](https://filles-et-maths.fr/2017/12/10/rjm-2017-lyon/) on this experience.
