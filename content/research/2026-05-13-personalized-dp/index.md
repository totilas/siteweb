description = "During my stay at the [Simons Institute](https://simons.berkeley.edu), I discovered that heterogeneous privacy budgets were quite popular, because of their nice interplay with data markets and incentives. However, I could not really convince myself that they were a good strategy. After some unsuccessful discussions with several participants, I was lucky to find an interested ear in Juba, who started to investigate this question with me. I am really happy to quantify this intuition in this paper: personalizing privacy budgets is often not worth the pain, but we also characterize when the gain is the largest."
