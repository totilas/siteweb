Rony is also a PhD student at Lille, and we met during the TV show Esprit sorcier. In parallel of his thesis, he is doing this very serious podcast about the reality of doing a PhD in France, covering a broad range of questions from his own experience. 

I participate to the emission dedicated to PhD female student in computer Science. The other panelists were [Marion Monier](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manon-mottier/), another PhD student at Lille University, [Helène Touzet](https://helene-touzet.cnrs.fr/), full professor and equality manager for the lab, and [Isabelle Colet](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabelle_Collet), the expert of gender equality in STEM.

You can listen to the [2-hour podcast](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/these-antithese-synthese/episodes/17---FOCUS--GENERAL---Faire-une-thse-en-sciences-du-numrique-quand-on-est-une-femme-e2g1jj1)
