I believe presenting research work to non-technical or more industry-oriented audiences is a true part of a researcher’s work, and also a very stimulating experience with questions that do not always arise in purely academic contexts. I also do my part for women’s visibility, in the hope of inspiring girls to join us!

*I receive many invitations to speak at events promoting women in science. I appreciate the intention, but I cannot accept them all, and I give priority to events where my contribution can make a real difference. If you are organizing such an event, please consider covering travel costs and offering speakers a fee: it helps share the effort fairly among the few women in the field.*
