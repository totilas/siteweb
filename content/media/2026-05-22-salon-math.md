I took part in the round table _Comment est fabriquée l'IA ?_ (How is AI made?) with Stéphane Mallat, moderated by Charlotte Mauger, at the 27th edition of the [Salon Culture et Jeux Mathématiques](https://salon-math.fr/espace-evenementiel/). For four days, the Salon brings mathematics to a broad audience on the place Saint-Sulpice in Paris, with this year's theme "Égalités".

You can watch the [recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWJb7s4Gjgk) of the round table (in French).
