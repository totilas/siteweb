I co-organize with Rachel Cummings, Christoph Lampert, Peter Kairouz, Nikita P. Kalinin and Amartya Sanyal a PPML workshop! We are part of the 18 selected workshops (among 52 proposals). 

Despite the tight schedule (acceptance notification on September 12th), we were able to accept 24 short papers after a double-blind review process (with all papers with at least two reviews). 

I also thank the six invited speakers who accepted to join us: Aurélien Bellet, Tamalika Mukherjee, Antti Honkela, Rasmus Pagh, Catuscia Palamidessi and Sahra Ghalebikesabi.

More details on our [website](https://ppml-eurips-2025.github.io/).
